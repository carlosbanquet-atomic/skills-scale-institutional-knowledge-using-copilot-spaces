# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and coach the organization in agile practices. They help the team self-organize and improve delivery flow.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track velocity and team metrics
- Maintain sprint board hygiene and transparency
- Protect the team from unnecessary interruptions during sprint execution

### Goals
- Enable efficient, predictable delivery
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Improve team collaboration and psychological safety
- Maintain a sustainable pace for the team

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation
- Weekly retrospective reviews
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- One-on-ones with team members on impediments

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with the Project Manager to keep delivery cadence visible and aligned with milestones
- Supports Product Managers by helping the team clarify priorities, dependencies, and trade-offs during planning
- Helps Developers remove obstacles so they can focus on delivering incrementally
- Shares team health and delivery indicators with stakeholders when appropriate

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads make critical architectural and design decisions, mentor team members, and ensure solutions meet quality and scalability standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review design proposals and technical solutions
- Provide technical mentorship and code review guidance
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure consistency with existing systems and standards
- Participate in estimation and technical planning

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Build a technically skilled and confident team
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risk
- Balance speed with solution quality

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings with the team
- Technical documentation and decision records
- Code review comments and mentorship
- Architecture and planning sessions

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to guide implementation decisions and maintain engineering standards
- Provides Product Managers with feasibility assessments and technical trade-off information
- Supports Project Managers by identifying delivery, dependency, and risk implications early
- Aligns with QA and DevOps to ensure the solution is testable, observable, and deployable

---

## QA Lead / Testing Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define testing strategy, oversee quality assurance activities, and ensure products meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for projects
- Create and maintain acceptance criteria with PMs and developers
- Oversee manual and automated testing execution
- Identify defects and coordinate resolution with the team
- Define quality gates and release readiness criteria
- Report quality metrics and test coverage outcomes

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality products that meet user and business expectations
- Reduce post-release defects and incidents
- Balance speed with thoroughness in testing
- Enable rapid feedback loops for continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions and strategy documents
- Defect reporting and triage meetings
- Quality metrics dashboards and reports
- Release readiness reviews

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Partners with Developers to validate fixes, identify regressions, and improve automation coverage
- Works with Product Managers and Business Analysts to confirm acceptance criteria are testable and complete
- Supports Project Managers with release gates, quality risk reporting, and launch readiness
- Coordinates with Stakeholders on quality risks that may affect go/no-go decisions

---

## DevOps Engineer / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain CI/CD infrastructure, manage deployments, and ensure systems are reliable, scalable, and observable in production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Automate testing, build, and deployment processes
- Manage infrastructure as code and deployment environments
- Monitor system health, performance, and security
- Implement rollback and disaster recovery procedures
- Support incident response and root cause analysis

### Goals
- Enable rapid, reliable deployments
- Minimize downtime and production incidents
- Maintain high observability and performance
- Reduce manual operational toil through automation

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination and deployment runbooks
- Infrastructure and pipeline documentation
- On-call incident response and post-mortems
- Monitoring and alerting configuration

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Coordinates with Developers to ensure applications are deployable, observable, and resilient in production
- Partners with Project Managers on deployment windows, rollback readiness, and release communication
- Shares operational risk signals with Product Managers and Stakeholders during milestone and incident reviews
- Aligns with QA on staging validation and production readiness checks

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience requirements, conduct research, and guide the team to deliver usable, intuitive products that meet customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and design specifications
- Define user flows and information architecture
- Collaborate with product on customer needs and feedback
- Review implementations against design specs
- Advocate for usability and accessibility standards

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and valuable user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Validate solutions with real user feedback
- Ensure quality from both a business and usability perspective

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and user research findings
- Design specifications and component libraries
- Usability testing sessions and feedback
- Collaboration with developers on implementation fidelity

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate user insight into product direction and prioritization
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure experiences are implemented consistently and accessibly
- Helps Project Managers understand user impact, dependency risks, and readiness for milestones
- Provides Stakeholders with customer value and experience insight that informs product decisions

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business requirements into actionable product and technical specifications, validate solutions, and ensure alignment between business needs and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Create detailed functional specifications and acceptance criteria
- Validate that solutions meet business objectives
- Identify business rules and edge cases
- Communicate between business stakeholders and technical teams
- Track requirements changes and manage scope clarity

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that solve the actual business problem
- Minimize rework and scope creep
- Ensure clear communication between business and technical teams
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering interviews and workshops
- Functional specification documents
- Acceptance criteria validation sessions
- Business requirements and traceability matrices

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Supports Product Managers by translating strategic priorities into clear requirements and trade-offs
- Helps Developers and QA by making expectations explicit and testable
- Assists Project Managers with scope clarification, dependency understanding, and milestone readiness
- Keeps Stakeholders informed on how requirements are being implemented and validated

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business and organizational interests, provide authority and resources, and make strategic decisions that impact project success.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve scope, budget, and timeline
- Make escalation decisions and resolve conflicts
- Provide strategic context and priority guidance
- Review and approve major deliverables
- Remove organizational and business blockers

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Make informed, timely decisions to keep projects on track
- Manage organizational dependencies and risks
- Communicate project status to leadership

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and status briefings
- Decision and approval meetings
- Executive summaries and business impact reports
- Escalation and risk communication

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers on outcome definition and prioritization
- Partners with Project Managers on milestone decisions, risks, and escalations
- Provides direction to Technical Leads and other delivery roles when trade-offs affect business outcomes
- Receives status updates from the team and helps unblock organizational issues affecting delivery
